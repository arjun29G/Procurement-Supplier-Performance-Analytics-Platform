# Procurement & Supplier Performance Analytics Platform

A data-driven procurement and supplier analytics platform designed to provide visibility into procurement spend, supplier performance, purchase-order cycle time, lead-time behavior, invoice reconciliation, inventory availability, and supplier risk.

The solution combines **Python, SQL Server, SQL, and Power BI** to transform large-scale procurement and supply-chain data into interactive business intelligence dashboards.

---

## Project Overview

The Procurement & Supplier Performance Analytics Platform analyzes procurement and related supply-chain data to help procurement teams:

- Monitor procurement spending
- Analyze supplier performance
- Evaluate purchase-order cycle time
- Analyze supplier lead-time behavior
- Identify lead-time variability
- Reconcile purchase orders and invoices
- Monitor inventory availability
- Identify low-inventory exposure
- Prioritize suppliers for further review
- Support procurement planning and decision-making

The platform processes more than **2.3 million purchase transaction records** and provides an interactive **Power BI dashboard with nine analytical pages**.

---

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of the project are:

- Improve procurement spend visibility
- Provide supplier-level performance analysis
- Identify procurement process delays
- Analyze supplier lead-time behavior
- Improve purchase and invoice reconciliation
- Provide inventory availability visibility
- Identify potential supplier risk areas
- Support data-driven procurement decisions
